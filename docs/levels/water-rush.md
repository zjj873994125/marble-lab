未来运动公式（当前未实现）为：

```text
theta_i(t) = asin(1.65 / 3.6) * sin(omega_i * t + phase_i)
head_i(t)  = [X_i, 7.9 - 3.6*cos(theta_i), 18 + 3.6*sin(theta_i)]
```
